The Sultanate of Sulu

The Sultanate of Sulu is a historic royal institution established in the 15th century in what is now the southern Philippines, with centuries of political, diplomatic, and cultural influence across the Sulu Archipelago and surrounding region. Its rulers maintained diplomatic relations with foreign powers and governed a maritime realm whose history remains an important part of Southeast Asia's heritage. Today, His Majesty Sultan Muedzul Lail Tan Kiram, a descendant of Sulu's historic ruling dynasty, carries forward the traditions and cultural legacy of the Sultanate, preserving an institution whose significance extends well beyond its former political authority.
